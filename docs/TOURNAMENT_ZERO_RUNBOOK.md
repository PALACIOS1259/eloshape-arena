# EloShape tournament-zero runbook

Updated: 2026-09-10

This is the operating checklist for the first invite-only EloShape tournament. Tournament zero is a rehearsal: use staging or an explicitly approved closed-beta production event, never synthetic QA data in production.

## Format and minimum field

- Traditional single-elimination team tournament.
- Exactly five active players per team.
- Minimum four complete teams: 20 active participants.
- Recommended rehearsal field: four teams; intended qualifier field: 16 teams.
- No entry fee, betting, gambling, virtual currency or paid feature.

Do not begin the event with fewer than 20 active participants. Cancel or reschedule instead.

## One week before

- [ ] Publish date, start time, check-in window, format, rules and support channel.
- [ ] Confirm every participant can sign in and receives EloShape email.
- [ ] Confirm each player has completed profile and location fields.
- [ ] Confirm each Riot ID, account level and Solo Queue rank were synced recently.
- [ ] Complete staff eligibility review for all players.
- [ ] Confirm every team has exactly five eligible active players and a captain.
- [ ] Export or screenshot the participant list as an operational backup.

## Event-day preflight

- [ ] Open registration/check-in only for the published window.
- [ ] Contact captains who have not checked in before the deadline.
- [ ] Lock entries and verify immutable roster snapshots.
- [ ] Confirm at least four checked-in complete teams remain.
- [ ] Generate the bracket once and inspect every seed and matchup.
- [ ] Publish the first-round matchups and expected round timing.
- [ ] Keep one staff member responsible for disputes and one communication channel of record.

## During the tournament

1. Captains play only the opponent shown in the published bracket.
2. A participant submits the match score and optional evidence.
3. The opposing side confirms the result or opens a dispute.
4. Staff reviews disputed evidence, records a reason, and resolves or dismisses the claim.
5. Verify the winner advanced to the correct next-round slot before the next match begins.

For a no-show, pause that branch of the bracket and document the decision in the support channel. Staff then selects the advancing side under **Walkover**, enters the required reason and verifies the audit trail. A walkover is stored as 0–0 and does not award a match win or loss.

## Finalization

- [ ] Confirm every non-bye bracket match has an official result and no dispute remains open.
- [ ] Verify champion, runner-up and placements against the bracket.
- [ ] Finalize the tournament once.
- [ ] Verify ranking ledgers, player/team points and Semi-Split qualification.
- [ ] Publish final results and explain any staff ruling.
- [ ] Save the audit-log export and incident notes.

## Post-event review

Within 24 hours, record:

- registration, email, Riot sync or check-in failures;
- incorrect roster or eligibility decisions;
- bracket/result/dispute issues;
- no-shows and response time;
- participant feedback and the exact fix owner.

Do not open the next event until every integrity-impacting issue has an owner and resolution.
